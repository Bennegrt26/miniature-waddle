Signal Calibration

Fit the signal model to readings taken in your own house.

How to take readings
  1. Pick one eero node. Stand in a straight line from it with nothing in between, at about 3, 10, 20 and 30 ft. Measure the distance with a tape measure.
  2. At each spot, read the signal 3–5 times and type all of them in that row, separated by commas. The average is used.
  3. Mac: hold Option and click the Wi-Fi icon, then use the RSSI value. Windows: run netsh wlan show interfaces and type the Signal value with a % sign, for example 78%. Android: use a Wi-Fi analyzer app.
  4. Make sure your device is connected to that node and not another one. The eero app shows which node each device is using.
  5. Keep to one Wi-Fi band (5 GHz is best) for all readings.
  6. Optional: add readings taken through a wall or floor and untick Use. Their Error column shows roughly how many dB that wall or floor costs.
